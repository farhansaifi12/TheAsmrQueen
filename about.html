About Chalk Crush

Chalk Crush is a free browser game from The Asmr Queen, the creator behind the dusty/crunchy ASMR videos on Instagram and YouTube — original content, no edits.

Tap or click a chalk block to crack it, hear the crunchy sound, build combos up to x8, and work through 10 levels from beginner to Dust Legend. The game has 16 chalk colours, 9 shapes, power tools including the Hammer, Roller and Power Drill, and rewards such as Dust Coins, Fever Mode, a Lucky Spin Wheel, Treasure Chests, badges and missions.
