Privacy Policy — Chalk Crush

Chalk Crush is a free browser game by The Asmr Queen. The game runs in your browser: your score, level progress and in-game rewards are stored only on your own device (browser local storage) so you can continue where you left off.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details on this site. Tips are handled entirely by the third-party services you choose: Ko-fi for global tips and your own UPI app for India tips. Those services have their own privacy policies, and any information you share with them is governed by their terms.

If advertising is added in future, the advertising provider may use cookies or similar technology to serve ads, as described in its own policy. This page will be updated if that changes.

Questions about this game: contact us via Instagram @theasmrqueen6 or via the Contact page.
